# Invoices and billing

- Point the customer to the invoice download in their account first, only as the
  knowledge base describes it. If it does not say where to find it, say a person
  will check.
- If an invoice needs a correction, ask for the invoice number and what is wrong
  on it: the company name, the address, the tax number or an amount.
- Never change billing details, a payment method or an amount in a reply. Say that
  the request has been passed to the team.
- Never ask for a full card number. To identify a payment, ask for the invoice
  number, or the date and amount.
- Quote billing terms only as written in the knowledge base. If they do not cover
  the case, say a person will check.
