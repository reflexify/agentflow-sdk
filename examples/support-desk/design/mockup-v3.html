What changed from v1, and where it comes from

  • One workspace in three panes. The queue stays beside the open ticket, so moving to the next one costs a keypress, not a page. Help Scout, Front, Plain
  • Presence and one writer at a time. Rings show who is viewing (amber) or replying (red), in the list and the header. When Hà is already replying, you can leave a note or Take over. The desk's own server tracks who has which ticket open, with no platform change. Help Scout, Front, Zendesk
  • Send pauses when something new arrives. If the customer wrote again after the draft, Send stops and offers Redraft with it or Send anyway. Help Scout collision stop, Zendesk
  • Send and set the status in one go, with your default on ⌘↵ and 10 seconds to undo. The undo window is in the desk: the send starts only once it ends. Zendesk "Submit as", Front, Freshdesk
  • The AI draft lives in the composer, with one-click redrafts (shorter, friendlier, formal, Vietnamese, your own instruction), Undo redraft, and the sources it used. No side panel to copy from. Zendesk auto assist, Plain, Intercom
  • Authorship stays visible. Sent replies say "AI draft, edited", "AI draft, unchanged" or "Sent by autopilot". Plain, Front
  • The / menu searches saved replies with a preview and fills in the customer's name. Help Scout, Front, Zendesk
  • Keyboard first. J/K to move, single keys for every action, ⌘K for anything, ? for the sheet, ⇧N for the next due ticket. Plain, Linear, Help Scout
  • A held-messages folder that says why each message was held, with Accept or Move inline. Zendesk suspended tickets
  • Autopilot screen. Per intent: mode, drafts, share sent unchanged, autopilot sends, and what people usually change. Here is where an intent moves from shadow to live. Plain shadow mode, Front, Intercom
  • A one-line summary on each ticket, in the queue and the customer's history. Contact notes shown beside every ticket. Front, Zendesk, Kustomer
  • Plan 3 now carries the data behind these screens:
    • intent and confidence on each draft;
    • a one-line ticket summary;
    • why a message was held;
    • whether a reply went out as the AI wrote it;
    • event notes for who did what;
    • note mentions and contact notes.
    The drafting agent also reads the team's notes, as context it never quotes.
  • Desk-only, built in Plan 4: presence, one writer at a time, paused sends, send-and-set-status with undo, the / menu, the @mention picker and Mentions folder, and the keyboard shortcuts.
